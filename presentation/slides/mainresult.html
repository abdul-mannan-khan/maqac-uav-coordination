Read each bar as MAQAC's score minus the other method's score, in percent. Against RAG, the strongest distributed rival, MAQAC is clearly higher in 8 of the 15 configurations, by 4 to 7 percent; in the other 7, including all disaster configurations, the two are statistically tied. The ties have two likely reasons: with two UAVs there is rarely a conflict for coordination to resolve, and in the disaster scenario all users sit in five tight clusters that both methods find and serve in much the same way.
