Baselines: RAG (IEEE T-RO 2025), two interference-aware deployments, He et al. (IEEE TNSM 2025), MAPPO with 5 seeds × 3,000 iterations and uncoordinated methods. Paired seeds, Holm-corrected tests.

Missions per level; link-limited = 4,320 comparison + 5,760 pre-registered stress missions

Every level answers a different question: the simulator compares methods at scale, the link-limited model tests how much the result depends on modelling choices, AirSim with PX4 checks that real flight control does not change the conclusions, and the hardware-in-the-loop runs check real-time feasibility on embedded hardware.
