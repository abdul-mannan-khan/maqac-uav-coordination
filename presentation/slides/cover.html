Vehicular Communications · revised submission

Certified coordination of UAV aerial base stations

under imperfect perception and backhaul delay

Abdul-Manan Khan1 · Rabia Nazir2 · Vishwanath Bijalwan3 · Venkatesan Vellaiyan4

1University of West London · 2University of Engineering and Technology Lahore
3Amity University Punjab · 4BITS Pilani Dubai Campus

MAQAC coordinates a fleet of UAVs that carry base stations after a disaster. The talk covers the problem, the three results on the theory side, and the evaluation from a network simulator up to a Pixhawk flight controller in the loop, including where the method does not win.
