Distributed submodular methods such as RAG have guarantees but negotiate over several message rounds and assume exact information. Learning methods such as MAPPO handle urgency but give no guarantee. Conformal methods calibrate perception but do not certify the coordination itself. MAQAC is the only approach with all five properties.
