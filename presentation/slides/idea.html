The method

Every second, one exchange of records, then every UAV decides

1 · Share

Flood service records: who was served, when

2 · Value

Detections + records + density prior, weighted by priority and survival

3 · Choose

Best footprint, soft partition, co-channel exclusion

4 · Fly

Move, admit users by SINR, rate and capacity

MAQAC

All UAVs choose at the same time: no message inside the round.

MAQAC-S

UAVs choose in turn; each announces its choice once.

Algorithm 1 and Section 4 of the paper

The loop runs once per second. Record sharing is the component that matters most: removing it cost 26 to 33 percent of the score in the ablation. The co-channel exclusion keeps UAVs on the same channel at least the reuse distance apart, derived from the link budget, without extra messages. MAQAC-S is the sequential variant used in the link-limited study.
