Result 2 · Theorem 4 and Lemma 7

A guarantee for the rule the UAVs run

g(G) ≥ β ⁄ (β + m) · g(O) − n(1 + β) ⁄ (β + m) · ε

m: observed overlap

How many chosen footprints improve the same user; known after the round. m ≤ 1 in 84–90% of 9,462 measured rounds.

β: soft partition

UAVs may leave their region at weight β. No disjoint footprints or hard partition assumed.

ε: estimation error

Split into a perception term and a backhaul-delay term, so both costs are visible.

The classical 1/2 is only a special case and is not claimed. 300 rounds and 100,000 adversarial instances: no violation.

G is the joint choice the fleet made in round k, O the best joint choice given the same history, n the number of UAVs. With disjoint footprints and exact values the bound reduces to the classical one half, which reviewers rightly said is not new; it is not claimed. The guarantee is per round, not for the whole mission.
