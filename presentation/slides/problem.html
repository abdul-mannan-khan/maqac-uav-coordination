The problem

After a disaster, the network has to fly

Urgent users first

A first-responder session survives 50 s of waiting with probability 0.25. Reaching users sooner is worth more than covering more area.

A lossy backhaul

The UAVs coordinate over their own multi-hop radio links. Messages are lost or late, and the fleet may share one channel.

Imperfect perception

Each UAV sees users only through its own detector, with misses, false alarms and delay.

Question: can the UAVs coordinate from local information, with a guarantee that holds for the rule they actually run?

Survival rates and classes: Section 3.3 of the paper

Three features make this different from classic coverage. Value decays with waiting time; the coordination channel is the fleet's own lossy backhaul; and what each UAV knows comes from a learned detector. The first submission assumed a fixed service order and disjoint footprints, which the deployed algorithm does not satisfy; this version removes both assumptions.
