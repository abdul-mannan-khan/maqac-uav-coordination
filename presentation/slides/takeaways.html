Takeaways

MAQAC is better, and certifiable

Better: 4–7% more urgent sessions than RAG and 8–13% more than MAPPO where UAVs compete for users, because it values waiting time.

Robust: up to 22% ahead of RAG when 40% of messages are lost, with 5–8× fewer messages, because it shares records instead of negotiating.

Certifiable: a per-round bound for the rule actually run, and a mission certificate that held in all 20 flown missions.

Real: PX4 flight in a city and a Pixhawk in the loop changed the score by less than 5%; decisions took 17–33 ms on a Jetson.

Code, results and videos: github.com/abdul-mannan-khan/maqac-uav-coordination · doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24113000

Each claim on this slide is backed by the results shown before: the main grid for the first, the message-loss study for the second, the certificate test and the flown missions for the third, and the AirSim and hardware-in-the-loop missions for the fourth.
