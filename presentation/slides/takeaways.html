Takeaways

Judge UAV coordination by what it needs from the network

  1. Time-stamped service events give urgency-aware coordination a guarantee that holds for simultaneous choice.
  2. The bound shows its own price: observed overlap, perception error and backhaul delay.
  3. A Learn-then-Test certificate held on test missions and chose where perception runs.
  4. One announcement per UAV matches a multi-round method on a reliable backhaul and beats it when messages are lost.
