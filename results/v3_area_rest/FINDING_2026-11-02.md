# v3 area rerun: MAQAC variants vs RAG (2 Nov 2026)

Data: `netsim_v3_area_rest.jsonl`, 4,140 missions.
- Setup: 1 km and 3 km areas; urban, suburban and disaster; 4/6/8 UAVs; 12 methods (no IAD at 3 km); seeds 20000–20019.
- Model: realistic model v3 with the 'sum' separation rule and Controller6kg.
- Run: vast.ai instance 58846709, 2 Nov, 16:13–19:00 UTC, 265 CPU-h, ≈ $1.9.
- Checks: 0 errors and 0 collisions (< 5 m) in all 4,140 missions.

Analysis: `scripts/analyze_v3_area_rest.py` → `vs_rag_summary.json`, `vs_rag_analysis.txt`. It compares paired J_session per cell with a 95 % CI and Holm over 18 cells. A "win" means a gain ≥ 3 % with the CI above 0 and Holm p < 0.05.

Consistency: the 3 km rows reproduce `results/rag_fix/` (same seeds and code path) to the printed digit.

Reading:
1. Plain MAQAC's gap to RAG appears only at 3 km. At 1 km it is level with RAG or slightly ahead (+1 to +2 %, significant in 5 cells).
2. Each fix closes the 3 km gap: turn-taking, RAG's rounds with MAQAC's rules, and the combined method. None leaves a significant loss in any of the 18 cells.
3. None beats RAG by the pre-registered 3 % margin, except maqac_rag in one cell. The supported claim is "matches RAG". "Beats RAG" is not supported.
4. Message cost (from the rag_fix test and the tuning screen; not saved in this file):
   - maqac_seq: 3–7 per round;
   - maqac_hyb: 12–58;
   - RAG and maqac_rag: 32–217.
   So maqac_seq matches RAG with about 10–30× fewer messages.
5. Where the combined method sits: it is closest to maqac_seq at 4 UAVs (hyb − seq between −0.1 and +0.4 %). It moves toward the RAG-rules variant at 3 km disaster with 6/8 UAVs (+1.4 and +1.6 % over seq). The tuning screen shows the conflict check firing in 96–99 % of rounds at 6 UAVs. These are tuning seeds, so treat this as indicative.

Theory note (corrected by the VehCom session, 2 Nov): choosing in turn gives Thm 3 with m = 1, i.e. β/(1+β) = 1/3 at β = 0.5, not 1/2. The ε term for information errors and consensus lag stays. m = 1 assumes lossless hand-over within a turn.

## Swap/stay full evaluation
Data: `netsim_v3_swap_stay.jsonl`, 1,440 missions.
- Methods: maqac_swap, maqac_seq_stay, maqac_swap_stay, rag_stay. Same 18 cells and seeds 20000–20019. Settings fixed beforehand on tuning seeds (swap eps 0.01, stay 0.1).
- Code: the VehCom frozen snapshot (`source_md5_swap_stay.txt`). Before the run, 6 area_rest rows reproduced exactly.
- Run: vast.ai 58881776, 64 cores, ≈ $1.1. Swap missions took about 14× as long as maqac_seq.
- Checks: 0 errors, 0 collisions.
Analysis: `with_swap_stay/vs_analysis.txt`, `with_swap_stay/vs_rag_summary.json`.

|---|---|---|---|
| maqac_swap vs rag | 0 | 0 | −1.5 to +2.2 % |

Reading: swaps and the stay bonus change MAQAC-S by under ±1.2 %. The tuning screen is confirmed on the evaluation seeds. Without communication stress the claim stays "MAQAC-S matches RAG". The stay bonus slightly hurts RAG, so MAQAC-S with the bonus is 0.6 to 2.5 % ahead of RAG with it, but always below the 3 % margin.
